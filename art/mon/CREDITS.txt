Emberdeep: painted monster art (art/mon/)

The monster pictures come from Flare, a free and open-source action RPG:
  https://github.com/flareteam/flare-game  (mods/fantasycore and mods/empyrean_campaign, images/enemies)
  https://flarerpg.org
Art by Clint Bellanger, Justin Jacobs and the Flare team (full list: Flare's CREDITS.txt).
  grunt  (Ghoul)            Flare's zombie
  archer (Bone Archer)      Flare's skeleton archer
  brute  (Minotaur)         Flare's minotaur
  boss0  (Varrok, Act 1)    Flare's skeleton knight boss
