Emberdeep: painted monster art (art/mon/)

The monster pictures come from Flare, a free and open-source action RPG:
  https://github.com/flareteam/flare-game  (mods/fantasycore and mods/empyrean_campaign, images/enemies)
  https://flarerpg.org
