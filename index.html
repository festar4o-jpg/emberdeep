How to play
• Touch anywhere on the left side and drag to move (push lightly to walk). Left-handed? Swap sides under Controls.
• Big button: basic attack (hold it). Small buttons: skills. Skills aim at the nearest enemy automatically.
• 🧪 heals you. Potions refill to 3 on every new depth; buy extras (up to 10) from the merchant. Belts can add potion charges.
• Gear: Main Hand, Off Hand (shields give armor and block), Helm, Body, Gloves, Belt, Boots, Amulet and two Rings.
• Maps (after beating Hell): the Waystone's 🗺 Map Device opens a map with a Sigil (Tier 1–15; stronger than Hell and climbing). A map is a zone with random modifiers (harder monsters or penalties for you, each adding Item Quantity and Rarity), champions and a map boss. Map drops roll their stats at T1–T3, and the Rarity from modifiers moves the odds toward T1. The Map Device's ? Help tab explains it all in short. Kill the boss to complete it: Sigils drop, often a tier higher. You have 3 lives per map; the town portal keeps it, other travel closes it. Craft Sigils with your currency at the Map Device. The Atlas (Map Device → Atlas) is a map of 60 nodes: run a Sigil on any revealed node, and completing it reveals its neighbours. Special nodes give their bonus the first time: Shrine (a blessing for the whole map), Vault (a hoard chest, more loot), Boss (a far tougher boss, much more loot), Corrupted (two extra modifiers, double rewards). Atlas points (from special nodes and every 4 new plain nodes) buy passives in the Atlas tree. Three sealed Citadels wait at the edges. Six Beacons (▲): clear one to light it, then place an Engraving in it (map bosses drop them) to change every map within 2 steps for several maps: more Quantity, champions, tougher bosses with more loot, currency, higher-tier Sigils, more monsters, or deeper (harder, richer) maps.
• The pinnacle: run a Sigil on a ♜ Citadel to fight its guardian (the Ashen Warden, the Rime Sentinel, the Rotted Matron), each dropping its Fragment of the Hollow Sun. With one of each, the Map Device opens the crater: THE HOLLOW SUN, four phases (a shield held by wisps, a collapsing crater, then fire jets), Eclipse (hide in the drifting shadows) and Gravity Well (run against the pull). Maps of Tier 6+ drop map uniques (two powers each; the strongest from Tier 11), and each Citadel guardian can drop its own. The Hollow Sun drops its own uniques (three powers each) and the Sunfire Crucible (+1 to All Skills burned into an item, once).
• Ember Rifts (after beating Hell): the Map Device's 🔥 Rift tab. Rift 1, 2, 3… with no top, each stronger. Kill monsters to fill the bar within 4 minutes (elites and champions fill more), then slay the Rift Guardian that appears. In time: the next level opens (2 levels with over half the time left, 3 with over two thirds). Dying costs 15 seconds. Monsters in a Rift drop half the usual items, but the Guardian drops more the higher the Rift (items, crafting orbs, gold), and the first clear of every 5th level gives a unique or set item. 📖 Book › Rifts shows each hero's best level and time.
• Crafting currency drops from monsters (more from champions and bosses, and in Nightmare and Hell) and is shared by all heroes (✦ Currency in the inventory). Use it at the ✦ Crafting Bench in any town (pick the item, then the currency) on a white, magic or rare item: Spark of Awakening (white → magic), Crown of Ascension (magic → rare, +1 stat), Shard of Accretion (+1 stat in a free slot), Cinder of Unmaking (reroll a rare), Tear of Fate (reroll values, keep tiers), Void Ash (remove a random stat), Star-Iron (one stat up a tier, rare). Delver's Awl adds a socket, the Socket Spindle rerolls empty sockets, the Whetstone adds 5% quality (up to 20%: more base damage or armor; these three also work on uniques and set items). Catalysts go on an item first and change its next Spark, Crown, Shard or Cinder: Precision (only T1–T3), Flame/Frost/Storms/Venom (a resistance), Bulwark (defence), Haste (speed), Fury (attack), Arcana (spell damage). Abyssal Embers (Hell, deeper endless depths and maps) corrupt an item: a quarter chance each of a corrupted implicit, a stat a tier up (uniques: a new power), every stat rerolled at T1–T3 (uniques: stats 15–25% stronger), or nothing; corrupted items can never be changed again. The Blacksmith's crafts still work too.
• Charms work from the charm pouch (9 slots: small 1, medium 2, large and grand 3). They can roll stats gear can't: Item Rarity, Gold Found, Experience, Life per Kill, Potion Healing and (large and grand) +1–2% maximum Resistances. Craft them with Shard, Cinder, Tear, Void Ash and Star-Iron (small 1 stat, medium 2, large 3, grand 2 plus its skill). Each act boss in Hell can drop its unique charm; carry one of each.
• Base kinds: helms (Greathelm: Life, Circlet: Cooldown, Hood: Crit), gloves (Gauntlets: Life on Hit, Wraps: Attack Speed, Bracers: Crit Damage), boots (Greaves: armor, Striders: speed, Treads: speed and regen), belts (Sash: potions, Girdle: Life, Chain Belt: Armor) and shields (Tower, Kite, Buckler) each have their own built-in stats. Maps of Tier 5+ can drop Mythic bases: 25% stronger and one extra Mythic stat.
• More stats: +% damage with one of your skills (weapons, foci, gloves, rings, amulets), +% damage to bosses, +% maximum Life, thorns (damage to attackers when hit), evasion (up to 40% of hits missed) and, on Mythic bases only, less damage taken (up to 30%).
• Stat tiers: every stat on a magic or rare item (and on charms) has a tier, T1 best … T9 worst, shown on the item card. Higher item levels can roll better tiers; Nightmare drops only T1–T6, Hell T1–T5, the Endless Depths after Hell T1–T3. A rare holds up to 2 prefixes (Life, Damage, Armor, Mana…) and 2 suffixes (Crit, speeds, resistances…). A rare full of T1–T2 stats rivals most uniques; uniques keep their special powers.
• Monsters hit with fire, cold, lightning or poison (coloured damage numbers). Resistances on armour, shields, rings, amulets and charms cut that part (max 75%). Nightmare lowers every resistance by 20%, Hell by 50%: see your stats screen.
• Status effects on you (badges with seconds left under your life bar): 🔥 Burning (fire, damage over time), ❄️ Chilled (cold, slowed), 🧊 Frozen (a big cold hit: you can't move for up to half a second, then not again for 3s), ⚡ Shocked (lightning, you take 15% more damage), ☠️ Poisoned (poison, stacking damage over time). The matching resistance makes them rarer and shorter; below 0% they come more often. Potions don't cure them: they wear off, and a new area clears them.
• Status effects on enemies (coloured dots over them): Ignite (fire skills, burning), Shock (lightning: +10% damage taken per stack, up to 3), Chill (cold: slowed; 4 cold hits Freeze), Poison (stacking damage over time; Thornstaffs poison), Bleed (axes and greataxes). Items can add "+% chance to Ignite / Shock / Poison" to every hit (weapons, foci, gloves, rings, amulets; up to 75%). Each skill on the ✨ screen says what its hits leave.
• Beat Act 5 to open Nightmare, then Hell (pick them at a Waystone). Harder monsters, but more and better loot: Nightmare x1.25 items, +25% Item Rarity, x1.5 gold; Hell x1.5 items, +50% Item Rarity, x2 gold, 4 zone boss items, extra act boss items, and a set or unique you haven't found yet within 120 kills (Normal 200, Nightmare 160).
• Warden: one-hand sword/axe/mace/dagger with a shield or a second one-hander (dual wield), or a two-hander that takes both hands. Axes bleed, maces stun, daggers are fast and crit.
• Arcanist: wand or scepter with an orb or tome, or a two-handed staff that boosts spell damage.
• Each portal takes you to town first. Walk to the merchant and Trade, then take the portal onward. Every act has its own town.
• 🌀 The Waystone in town takes you to any town or depth you have reached, with fresh monsters. On boss depths pick Everything, Boss only or No boss: farm a boss for its trophy unique and set pieces. 🏠 Return to Town (this menu) brings you back from anywhere.
• Walk over loot to pick it up. Open 🎒 to equip gear — a green ▲ means it's an upgrade.
• Each level gives a skill point (spend it in ✨), +5% damage and more Life. Max level is 50; after that XP fills Paragon levels (✦, blue XP bar), no cap.
• Passive points: 1 per level, 2 for the first kill of each act boss and 1 per Paragon level. Spend them in the passive tree (🌳): drag to move, pinch to zoom, tap a node to see it, then Take it. A node can be taken when it touches your start or a node you have. Choice nodes (diamonds) let you pick one of three bonuses. A node can be taken back for gold (more at higher levels) once nothing beyond it depends on it; take a choice node back and again to change its bonus. Keystones (the four big nodes at the edge) change the rules with a downside: Blood Magic (skills cost Life), Unwavering (no Freeze or slow, less damage taken, but no dodge), Glass Cannon (30% more damage, 30% less Life), Elemental Overload (double Ignite, Shock and Poison chances, no critical hits).
• Skill modifiers (✨ Skills tab): every skill has 3, unlocked at skill rank 3, 6 and 10. One can be on per skill; tap to swap any time, for free.
• Paths (✨ Path tab): at level 20 choose one of your class's two paths (Warden: Bulwark or Reaver; Arcanist: Stormcaller or Pyreborn). It is for good. Each path has 4 perks, at levels 20, 28, 36 and 44, and becomes your title.
• Settings › Performance: frame rate (30 saves battery), Sharpness, Particles, Lighting and Glows, each on its own, so every phone can find its balance. Battery saver uses the lightest of all four (and turns on by itself under 20% battery). Show FPS puts a counter at the top.
• Five acts of five depths: Sunken Crypts, Ashen Wastes, Rotwood Marsh, Frozen Citadel, The Ember Core. Depth 5 of each act is a boss, each tougher and richer than the last. Beat the Cinder King to unlock the Endless Depths. Marsh poison slows you; Ember Core lava burns.
• Each act has its own monsters. Elite packs glow with a coloured ring and carry 1–3 affixes (more in later acts; always 3 in the Endless Depths): Fast (moves and attacks faster), Shielded (a bubble that makes it immune for a moment), Vampiric (heals when it hits you), Explodes on Death (its corpse blasts a moment later: step away), Molten (leaves a trail of fire), Teleporter (blinks next to you), Waller (raises stone walls), Frozen Pulse (frost strikes on you that slow), Summoner (calls in minions), Reflects Damage (part of your hits comes back), Storm-Charged (three lightning strikes around you that can Shock you) and Venomous (poison hits, and a poison cloud where you stand). Marked circles on the ground are where a strike lands: dodge out.
• Find the purple portal and press Descend.
• 💨 Dodge (right thumb, next to the skills; 🧪 sits right above it) dashes you out of danger, untouchable for a moment.
• Keyboard: WASD to move, J/1–5 skills, Space/Shift dodge, Q potion, I bag, K skills, E trade/descend.
